Clinical

CategoryDefinition

T0No evidence of a primary tumor.
TisCarcinoma in situ (intraductal tumor).

T1aSolitary tumor 5 cm or less in greatest dimension, without vascular invasion.
T1bSolitary tumor more than 5 cm in greatest dimension, without vascular invasion.
T2Solitary tumor with intrahepatic vascular invasion, or multiple tumors (with or without vascular invasion).

Notes

Regional nodes depend on tumor side: for right-sided tumors they are the hilar, periduodenal and peripancreatic nodes; for left-sided tumors the hilar, gastrohepatic and inferior phrenic nodes. The 8th edition removed periductal (infiltrating) growth pattern from the T4 definition used in the 7th edition.

Reference only — staging must be confirmed against the full AJCC 8th edition chapter and the patient's complete clinical picture.
