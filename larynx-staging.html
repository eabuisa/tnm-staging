T1Supraglottis: tumor limited to one subsite of the supraglottis with normal vocal cord mobility.
T1Glottis: tumor limited to the vocal cord(s), may involve the anterior or posterior commissure, with normal mobility (see T1a and T1b).
T1Subglottis: tumor limited to the subglottis.
T1aGlottis only: tumor limited to one vocal cord, with normal mobility.
T1bGlottis only: tumor involves both vocal cords, with normal mobility.
T2Supraglottis: tumor invades the mucosa of more than one adjacent subsite of the supraglottis or glottis, or a region outside the supraglottis (for example, mucosa of the base of the tongue, vallecula, or medial wall of the pyriform sinus), without fixation of the larynx.
T2Glottis: tumor extends to the supraglottis and/or subglottis, and/or there is impaired vocal cord mobility.
T2Subglottis: tumor extends to the vocal cord(s) with normal or impaired mobility.
T3Supraglottis: tumor is limited to the larynx with vocal cord fixation and/or invades any of the following: postcricoid area, pre-epiglottic space, paraglottic space, and/or inner cortex of the thyroid cartilage.
T3Glottis: tumor is limited to the larynx with vocal cord fixation and/or invasion of the paraglottic space and/or inner cortex of the thyroid cartilage.
T3Subglottis: tumor is limited to the larynx with vocal cord fixation and/or invasion of the paraglottic space and/or inner cortex of the thyroid cartilage.
T4aModerately advanced local disease. Supraglottis: tumor invades through the outer cortex of the thyroid cartilage and/or invades tissues beyond the larynx (for example, trachea, soft tissues of the neck including the deep extrinsic muscle of the tongue, strap muscles, thyroid, or esophagus).
T4aModerately advanced local disease. Glottis: tumor invades through the outer cortex of the thyroid cartilage and/or invades tissues beyond the larynx (for example, trachea, cricoid cartilage, soft tissues of the neck including the deep extrinsic muscle of the tongue, strap muscles, thyroid, or esophagus).

N categories

Clinical

CategoryDefinition
NXRegional lymph nodes cannot be assessed.
N0No regional lymph node metastasis.
N1Metastasis in a single ipsilateral lymph node, 3 cm or smaller in greatest dimension, with no extranodal extension (ENE-negative).
N2aMetastasis in a single ipsilateral lymph node, more than 3 cm but no more than 6 cm in greatest dimension, with no extranodal extension (ENE-negative).
N2bMetastases in multiple ipsilateral lymph nodes, none more than 6 cm in greatest dimension, with no extranodal extension (ENE-negative).
N2cMetastases in bilateral or contralateral lymph nodes, none more than 6 cm in greatest dimension, with no extranodal extension (ENE-negative).
N3aMetastasis in a lymph node more than 6 cm in greatest dimension, with no extranodal extension (ENE-negative).
N3bMetastasis in any lymph node(s) with clinically overt extranodal extension (ENE-positive).

Pathologic

CategoryDefinition
NXRegional lymph nodes cannot be assessed.
N0No regional lymph node metastasis.
N1Metastasis in a single ipsilateral lymph node, 3 cm or smaller in greatest dimension, with no extranodal extension (ENE-negative).
N2aMetastasis in a single ipsilateral lymph node 3 cm or smaller in greatest dimension with extranodal extension (ENE-positive), or a single ipsilateral lymph node more than 3 cm but no more than 6 cm in greatest dimension with no extranodal extension (ENE-negative).
N2bMetastases in multiple ipsilateral lymph nodes, none more than 6 cm in greatest dimension, with no extranodal extension (ENE-negative).
N2cMetastases in bilateral or contralateral lymph nodes, none more than 6 cm in greatest dimension, with no extranodal extension (ENE-negative).
N3aMetastasis in a lymph node more than 6 cm in greatest dimension, with no extranodal extension (ENE-negative).
N3bMetastasis in a single ipsilateral lymph node more than 3 cm in greatest dimension with extranodal extension (ENE-positive), or multiple ipsilateral, contralateral, or bilateral lymph nodes, any with ENE-positive, or a single contralateral lymph node of any size with ENE-positive.
