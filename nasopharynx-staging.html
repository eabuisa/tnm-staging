Nasopharynx — TNM Staging (AJCC 8th)

T, N and M category definitions and stage groups for nasopharynx from the AJCC 8th edition.

T categories

Clinical

CategoryDefinition
TXThe primary tumor cannot be assessed.
T0No primary tumor is found, but a cervical (neck) lymph node is involved by EBV-positive tumor (Epstein-Barr virus-positive nodal disease with no identifiable primary tumor).
TisCarcinoma in situ: cancer cells are present without invasion into deeper tissues.
T1Tumor is confined to the nasopharynx, or extends into the oropharynx and/or nasal cavity, without involvement of the parapharyngeal space.
T2Tumor extends into the parapharyngeal space, and/or involves adjacent soft tissues: the medial pterygoid muscle, the lateral pterygoid muscle, or the prevertebral muscles.
T3Tumor invades bony structures — the base of the skull, a cervical vertebra, or the pterygoid structures — and/or invades the paranasal sinuses.
T4Tumor extends inside the skull (intracranial extension), involves cranial nerves, the hypopharynx, the orbit, or the parotid gland, and/or infiltrates soft tissue extensively beyond the lateral (outer) surface of the lateral pterygoid muscle.

N categories

Clinical

CategoryDefinition
NXThe regional lymph nodes cannot be assessed.
N0No spread to regional lymph nodes.
N1Spread to cervical (neck) lymph nodes on one side only, and/or to retropharyngeal lymph nodes on one or both sides (retropharyngeal nodes count as N1 regardless of laterality). The involved nodes are 6 cm or smaller in greatest dimension and lie above the caudal (lower) border of the cricoid cartilage.
N2Spread to cervical (neck) lymph nodes on both sides. The involved nodes are 6 cm or smaller in greatest dimension and lie above the caudal (lower) border of the cricoid cartilage.
N3Spread to cervical (neck) lymph nodes on one or both sides that are larger than 6 cm in greatest dimension, and/or nodal spread extending below the caudal (lower) border of the cricoid cartilage.

Notes

Applies to nasopharyngeal carcinoma. Nasopharynx cancer is treated primarily with radiation (with or without chemotherapy) rather than surgery, so AJCC 8th edition provides no separate pathologic (pTNM) classification for this site: the same T, N and M definitions and the same stage groups are used for clinical and pathologic lists. T0 is a special category for an EBV-positive neck node with no primary tumor found. Note that a 9th version of AJCC/UICC staging for nasopharyngeal carcinoma has since been published; this file follows the 8th edition. Combinations with no defined stage group in AJCC 8th (for example T0 N0, or Tis with any nodal spread) are intentionally not assigned a stage.

Reference only — staging must be confirmed against the full AJCC 8th edition chapter and the patient's complete clinical picture.
