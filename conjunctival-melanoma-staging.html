Notes

No stage grouping is recommended for conjunctival melanoma in AJCC 8th edition; T, N and M are recorded without a stage group. A quadrant is defined by clock hours, starting at the limbus and extending from the central cornea to beyond the eyelid margins. Clinical T is based on location and quadrants involved; pathologic T for T1/T2 tumors is based on thickness (the 8th edition uses a single 2.0 mm cutoff — the 0.5/1.5 mm tiers in some references are from the 7th edition). In 8th-edition tables, Tis appears in the pathologic T list only.

Reference only — staging must be confirmed against the full AJCC 8th edition chapter and the patient's complete clinical picture.
