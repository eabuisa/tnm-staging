Notes

Same T/N/M value lists used for clinical and pathologic; in AJCC 8th tables the T2a/T2b and T3a/T3b subdivisions are designated pT (pathologic). Parent T4 is listed for completeness but is not used in stage groups, where T4a/T4b are specified. Parent M1 (unspecified) has no stage group: IVA vs IVB depends on M1a vs M1b. T4a description includes seminal vesicles (ACS/Alberta/PMC sources); NCI PDQ and CAP list T4a as prostatic stroma/uterus/vagina, with seminal vesicles under parent T4 - see verification report.
