Notes

Two classification bases — use the toggle above. AJCC Stage: the AJCC 8th-edition prognostic stage groups, which require the TNM category PLUS the pre-treatment PSA and the Grade Group, so the stage-group entries also match on those inputs. Any Grade Group 5 tumor without nodal or distant spread is stage IIIC, node-positive (N1) M0 disease is stage IVA, and any M1 disease is stage IVB, regardless of the T/PSA-based rows; in the stage I and IIA entries the bare value T2 means pathologic pT2 (organ-confined at prostatectomy), while clinical matches use the cT values. If PSA or Grade Group is unavailable, AJCC advises assigning the group from whichever factors are known. NCCN Risk Group: the NCCN risk stratification for clinically localized disease (NCCN Prostate Cancer guidelines, Version 3.2026, PROS-2). Unlike an AJCC stage group, an NCCN risk group is a clinical, pre-treatment classification — it is built from the clinical T category, PSA, Grade Group, and biopsy core involvement, and it is the grouping that drives treatment selection for localized disease. NCCN lists node-positive (N1) M0 disease as Regional and M1 disease as Metastatic, separate from the localized risk groups. The NCCN groups changed recently, and this app follows the current table: since Version 1.2025 the high-risk features are cT3–cT4, Grade Group 4 or 5, or PSA > 20 ng/mL, and very high risk requires at least two of those three features with the PSA threshold raised to > 40 ng/mL (primary Gleason pattern 5 and more than 4 cores with Grade Group 4–5 cancer no longer define very high risk on their own); since Version 1.2026 the very-low-risk group has been removed, so a tumor that met the old very-low criteria is now classified as Low.

Reference only — staging must be confirmed against the full AJCC 8th edition chapter and the patient's complete clinical picture.
