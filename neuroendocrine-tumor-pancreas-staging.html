Notes

Applies to well-differentiated neuroendocrine tumors (G1, G2, and well-differentiated G3). Poorly differentiated neuroendocrine carcinoma (NEC) is staged using the carcinoma chapter for this organ, not this chapter. Grade (mitotic count and Ki-67 index) is recorded separately and does not change the stage group. 'Limited to the pancreas' means no invasion of adjacent organs or large-vessel walls; spread into peripancreatic fat alone does not upstage the tumor. Size cutoffs: T1 is under 2 cm and T2 is 2–4 cm (some secondary sources write T1 as 2 cm or smaller, following the 7th edition/ENETS wording). For multiple synchronous tumors, use the highest T category and add the (m) suffix (or the tumor count, e.g. T3(2)).

Reference only — staging must be confirmed against the full AJCC 8th edition chapter and the patient's complete clinical picture.
