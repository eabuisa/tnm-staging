Clinical

CategoryDefinition
TXPrimary tumor cannot be assessed.
T0No evidence of a primary tumor.
TisCarcinoma in situ (vaginal intraepithelial neoplasia, grade 3). Not part of the FIGO system.
T1Tumor confined to the vagina (parent category; see T1a and T1b).
T1aTumor confined to the vagina and 2 cm or smaller in greatest dimension.
T1bTumor confined to the vagina and larger than 2 cm in greatest dimension.
T2Tumor invades the tissues beside the vagina (paravaginal tissues) but has not reached the pelvic wall (parent category; see T2a and T2b).
T2aTumor invades paravaginal tissues but not the pelvic wall, and is 2 cm or smaller.
T2bTumor invades paravaginal tissues but not the pelvic wall, and is larger than 2 cm.
T3Tumor extends to the pelvic wall and/or causes a blocked kidney (hydronephrosis) or a nonfunctioning kidney.
T4Tumor invades the lining (mucosa) of the bladder or rectum, or extends beyond the true pelvis. Bullous edema alone does not qualify.

Notes

Staging is primarily clinical; tumors that began in the cervix or vulva and extend into the vagina are staged as cervical or vulvar cancers, not vaginal. The 2 cm size subdivisions (T1a/T1b, T2a/T2b) follow the FIGO 2018 update as incorporated by AJCC; FIGO itself does not subdivide stages I and II, so FIGO stage I covers AJCC IA and IB, and FIGO stage II covers IIA and IIB.

Reference only — staging must be confirmed against the full AJCC 8th edition chapter and the patient's complete clinical picture.
