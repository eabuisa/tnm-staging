Notes

Only the anatomic stage groups (I-IV) are encoded here. Treatment selection also requires the separate WHO/FIGO prognostic risk score (based on age, antecedent pregnancy, interval, pretreatment hCG, tumor size, metastasis site and number, and prior chemotherapy); FIGO subdivides stages I-IV into A (low risk, score 6 or less) and B (high risk, score 7 or more) using that score. There is no N category in GTN staging, and a parent M1 without the a/b subcategory cannot be assigned a single stage group.

Reference only — staging must be confirmed against the full AJCC 8th edition chapter and the patient's complete clinical picture.
