Notes

AJCC 8th kidney has a single positive N category (N1; no N2/N3) and no M subcategories (M0/M1 only). Clinical and pathologic T/N/M lists and stage groups are the same. Parent categories T1/T2/T3 are included in both the lists and the stage groups alongside their subcategories.
