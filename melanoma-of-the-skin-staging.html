Notes

Stage groups in this file are the PATHOLOGIC (pTNM) prognostic stage groups; clinical stage groups differ (clinically, T1b N0 M0 is stage IB rather than IA, and clinical stage III is not subdivided into IIIA-IIID). T is Breslow thickness, recorded to the nearest 0.1 mm, plus ulceration; mitotic rate is not a T criterion in the 8th edition. Parent values T1-T4, N1-N3 and M1 are the unspecified groupings (ulceration, detection mode or site/LDH unknown or unspecified) and are not used in the stage-group matches, which use the lettered subcategories. N: suffix 'a' = clinically occult (found only on sentinel-node biopsy / microscopic examination), 'b' = clinically detected, 'c' = in-transit, satellite and/or microsatellite metastases are present. AJCC publishes a single N definition table used for both clinical and pathologic N, so the clinical and pathologic N lists here are identical; in practice the 'a' (occult) subcategories can only be assigned after sentinel-node biopsy or other pathologic node assessment. The clinical and pathologic T and M lists are likewise identical. M: each M1 subcategory takes an LDH suffix in AJCC notation - (0) when serum LDH is not elevated and (1) when it is elevated, e.g. M1a(1); a plain M1a/M1b/M1c/M1d value here means LDH not recorded or unspecified, and the suffix does not change the stage group (stage IV).
