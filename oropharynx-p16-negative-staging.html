T categories

Clinical

CategoryDefinition
TXPrimary tumor cannot be assessed.
TisCarcinoma in situ.
T1Tumor 2 cm or smaller in greatest dimension.
T2Tumor larger than 2 cm but not larger than 4 cm in greatest dimension.
T3Tumor larger than 4 cm in greatest dimension, or extension to the lingual surface of the epiglottis.
T4Moderately advanced (T4a) or very advanced (T4b) local disease. See T4a and T4b.
T4aModerately advanced local disease: tumor invades the larynx, extrinsic muscle of the tongue, medial pterygoid muscle, hard palate, or mandible. Mucosal extension to the lingual surface of the epiglottis from a base-of-tongue or vallecula primary does not count as invasion of the larynx.
T4bVery advanced local disease: tumor invades the lateral pterygoid muscle, pterygoid plates, lateral nasopharynx, or skull base, or encases the carotid artery.

Notes

From the AJCC 8th chapter 'Oropharynx (p16-) and Hypopharynx'; only the OROPHARYNX T definitions are used here — the hypopharynx T definitions in the same chapter are subsite-based and are not included (simplification). There is no T0 category in this chapter (T0 with a p16-positive node is staged in the HPV-mediated chapter; a p16-negative unknown primary uses a different chapter). Unlike p16-positive disease, extranodal extension (ENE) changes the N category here. Clinical and pathologic stage groups are the same table, so no separate pathologic stage groups are listed. Parent T4, N2, and N3 entries are listed for completeness but are not used in the stage groups, where T4a/T4b, N2a/N2b/N2c, and N3a/N3b are specified.

Reference only — staging must be confirmed against the full AJCC 8th edition chapter and the patient's complete clinical picture.
