T categories

Clinical

CategoryDefinition
TXPrimary tumor cannot be assessed.
T0No evidence of a primary tumor.
TisCarcinoma in situ.
T1Tumor limited to the ampulla of Vater or sphincter of Oddi, or invading beyond the sphincter of Oddi - see T1a and T1b.
T1aTumor limited to the ampulla of Vater or sphincter of Oddi.
T1bTumor invades beyond the sphincter of Oddi (perisphincteric invasion) and/or into the duodenal submucosa.
T2Tumor invades into the muscularis propria of the duodenum.
T3Tumor invades the pancreas or extends into peripancreatic or periduodenal tissue or duodenal serosa, without involvement of the celiac axis or superior mesenteric artery - see T3a and T3b.
T3aTumor directly invades the pancreas, up to 0.5 cm.
T3bTumor extends more than 0.5 cm into the pancreas, or extends into peripancreatic or periduodenal tissue or duodenal serosa, without involvement of the celiac axis or superior mesenteric artery.
T4Tumor involves the celiac axis, superior mesenteric artery, and/or common hepatic artery, irrespective of tumor size.

Notes

New in the 8th edition: T1 is split at the sphincter of Oddi (T1a versus T1b) and pancreatic invasion is split at 0.5 cm (T3a versus T3b); the N category is by node count (N1 = 1-3, N2 = 4 or more).
