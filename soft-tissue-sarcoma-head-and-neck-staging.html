CategoryDefinition

Notes

AJCC 8th edition provides NO prognostic stage groups for soft tissue sarcoma of the head and neck (per NCI PDQ), so only the T, N, M and grade categories are recorded. Grade is the FNCLCC score: tumor differentiation (1-3) + mitotic count (1-3) + necrosis (0-2); total 2-3 = G1, 4-5 = G2, 6-8 = G3. The PDQ/AJCC T table for this chapter lists no T0 category.

Reference only — staging must be confirmed against the full AJCC 8th edition chapter and the patient's complete clinical picture.
