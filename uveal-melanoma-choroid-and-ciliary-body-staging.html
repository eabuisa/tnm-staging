Notes

The size category (1-4) is set by tumor thickness and largest basal diameter together; the letter suffix records ciliary body involvement and extraocular extension. Iris melanoma has its own T categories and no stage groups — see the separate Iris Melanoma entry. Histologic type (spindle/mixed/epithelioid) is recorded as grade in this chapter but does not change the anatomic stage group.

Reference only — staging must be confirmed against the full AJCC 8th edition chapter and the patient's complete clinical picture.
