T categories

Clinical

CategoryDefinition
TXPrimary tumor cannot be assessed.
T3Tumor limited to the mucosa and the immediately underlying superficial soft tissues, regardless of thickness or greatest dimension. There are no T1 or T2 categories for this site.
T4aModerately advanced disease: tumor involves deep soft tissue, cartilage, bone, or overlying skin.
T4bVery advanced disease: tumor involves brain, dura, skull base, lower cranial nerves (IX, X, XI, XII), masticator space, carotid artery, prevertebral space, or mediastinal structures.

Notes

Staging for mucosal melanoma of the head and neck starts at T3 and Stage III because even small, superficial tumors behave aggressively - there are no T1 or T2 categories and no Stages I or II. The same T, N, and M definitions and the same stage groups are used for clinical and pathologic staging, so there is no separate stageGroupsPathologic. One secondary source (a Chinese guideline appendix) misplaced lower cranial nerve, masticator space, carotid artery, prevertebral space, and mediastinal involvement under T4a; all other sources checked (PathologyOutlines, ICCR dataset, and two PMC reviews) place those structures in T4b, as used here.

Reference only — staging must be confirmed against the full AJCC 8th edition chapter and the patient's complete clinical picture.
