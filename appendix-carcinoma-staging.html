T categories

Clinical

CategoryDefinition
TXPrimary tumor cannot be assessed.
T0No evidence of a primary tumor.
TisCarcinoma in situ (intramucosal carcinoma): invasion of the lamina propria or extension into, but not through, the muscularis mucosae.
Tis(LAMN)Low-grade appendiceal mucinous neoplasm (LAMN) confined by the muscularis propria; acellular mucin or mucinous epithelium may extend into the muscularis propria. T1 and T2 do not apply to LAMN.
T1Tumor invades the submucosa (through the muscularis mucosae but not into the muscularis propria). Not applicable to LAMN.
T2Tumor invades the muscularis propria. Not applicable to LAMN.
T3Tumor invades through the muscularis propria into the subserosa or the mesoappendix. For LAMN, acellular mucin or mucinous epithelium reaching the subserosa is also T3.
T4Tumor invades the visceral peritoneum (including acellular mucin or mucinous epithelium on the serosa of the appendix or mesoappendix) and/or directly invades adjacent organs or structures - see T4a and T4b.
T4aTumor penetrates the visceral peritoneum, including acellular mucin or mucinous epithelium involving the serosa of the appendix or the serosa of the mesoappendix. For LAMN, acellular mucin or mucinous epithelium reaching the serosa is T4a.
T4bTumor directly invades or adheres to adjacent organs or structures.

Notes

This chapter covers appendiceal adenocarcinoma and its variants, goblet cell adenocarcinoma, mucinous neoplasms (including LAMN and HAMN), and small/large cell neuroendocrine carcinoma; well-differentiated neuroendocrine tumors use a separate chapter and are NOT staged here. Grade (the extra input) affects only the stage IV grouping of intraperitoneal disease: M1a is always stage IVA, M1b is IVA when G1 and IVB when G2, G3 or GX, and M1c is IVC. HAMN is staged with the adenocarcinoma T categories (Tis(LAMN) does not apply to HAMN); for LAMN, acellular mucin or mucinous epithelium in the subserosa is T3 and on the serosa is T4a.

Reference only — staging must be confirmed against the full AJCC 8th edition chapter and the patient's complete clinical picture.
