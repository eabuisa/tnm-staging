Notes

Stage groups combine TNM with the post-orchiectomy serum marker category (S). S1 requires ALL of: LDH less than 1.5 times the upper limit of normal, hCG below 5,000 mIU/mL, and AFP below 1,000 ng/mL. S2 is any ONE of: LDH 1.5-10 times normal, hCG 5,000-50,000 mIU/mL, or AFP 1,000-10,000 ng/mL. S3 is any ONE of: LDH above 10 times normal, hCG above 50,000 mIU/mL, or AFP above 10,000 ng/mL. Except for Tis and T4, the T category is assigned from the radical orchiectomy specimen, so the pathologic T list is the working list. Discontinuous tumor in the spermatic cord through vascular invasion is classified as M1, not T3. The umbrella groups Stage I, II, and III are defined in AJCC with SX (markers not done); because this calculator requires an S value, those combinations resolve to their substages instead (for example, nodal disease with S2 is IIIB and with S3 is IIIC).

Reference only — staging must be confirmed against the full AJCC 8th edition chapter and the patient's complete clinical picture.
