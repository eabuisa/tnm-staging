T categories

Clinical

CategoryDefinition
TXThe primary tumor cannot be assessed.
T0There is no evidence of a primary tumor.
T1aA single (solitary) tumor 2 cm or smaller in greatest size, whether or not vascular invasion is present.
T1bA single (solitary) tumor larger than 2 cm, with no vascular invasion.
T2A single (solitary) tumor larger than 2 cm with vascular invasion, or more than one tumor with none larger than 5 cm.
T3More than one tumor, with at least one tumor larger than 5 cm.
T4A single tumor or multiple tumors of any size that involve a major branch of the portal vein or hepatic vein, or a tumor that grows directly into a neighboring organ other than the gallbladder, or a tumor that perforates the visceral peritoneum (breaks through the covering of the liver).

Notes

Applies to hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) only; intrahepatic cholangiocarcinoma has a separate AJCC 8th edition chapter and staging system. AJCC TNM staging for HCC is separate from the Barcelona Clinic Liver Cancer (BCLC) system: BCLC also incorporates liver function and performance status and links stages to treatment, whereas AJCC TNM describes only the anatomic extent of the tumor and does not take liver function into account. T1 is an umbrella category (a solitary tumor 2 cm or smaller, or a solitary tumor larger than 2 cm without vascular invasion) that is always subdivided into T1a or T1b for stage grouping, so T1 alone is not listed as a selectable category. In AJCC 8th, a solitary tumor 2 cm or smaller is T1a whether or not vascular invasion is present; vascular invasion only begins to affect the T category for solitary tumors larger than 2 cm. A background liver fibrosis score, based on the Ishak scoring system, is recorded for prognostic purposes (F0 = Ishak score 0-4, none to moderate fibrosis; F1 = Ishak score 5-6, severe fibrosis or cirrhosis), but it does not change the AJCC stage group. Histologic grade is likewise recorded separately and does not change the stage group.
