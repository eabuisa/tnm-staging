Cervix Uteri — TNM Staging (AJCC 8th)

T, N and M category definitions and stage groups for cervix uteri from the AJCC 8th edition.

T categories

Clinical

CategoryDefinition
TXPrimary tumor cannot be assessed.
T0No evidence of a primary tumor.
TisCarcinoma in situ (preinvasive carcinoma); abnormal cells are confined to the surface epithelium without invasion.
T1Carcinoma is strictly confined to the cervix. Extension into the uterine corpus is disregarded.
T1aInvasive carcinoma that can be diagnosed only by microscopy, with a maximum depth of stromal invasion of less than 5 mm. Involvement of lymphovascular spaces does not change the category, and the horizontal (lateral) extent of the lesion is no longer considered.
T1a1Microscopic carcinoma with measured stromal invasion less than 3 mm in depth.
T1a2Microscopic carcinoma with measured stromal invasion 3 mm or more and less than 5 mm in depth.
T1bInvasive carcinoma with a measured deepest invasion of 5 mm or more, still limited to the cervix.
T1b1Invasive carcinoma 5 mm or more in depth of stromal invasion and 2 cm or less in greatest dimension.
T1b2Invasive carcinoma more than 2 cm and 4 cm or less in greatest dimension.
T1b3Invasive carcinoma more than 4 cm in greatest dimension.
T2Carcinoma extends beyond the cervix/uterus, but has not reached the pelvic wall or the lower third of the vagina.
T2aSpread is limited to the upper two-thirds of the vagina, without parametrial invasion.
T2a1Upper vaginal involvement without parametrial invasion; tumor less than 4 cm in greatest dimension.
T2a2Upper vaginal involvement without parametrial invasion; tumor 4 cm or more in greatest dimension.
T2bParametrial invasion is present, but the tumor does not reach the pelvic wall.
T3Carcinoma involves the lower third of the vagina and/or extends to the pelvic wall and/or causes hydronephrosis or a nonfunctioning kidney. Pelvic or para-aortic lymph node spread is classified under the N category and results in stage IIIC.
T3aCarcinoma involves the lower third of the vagina, with no extension to the pelvic wall.
T3bExtension to the pelvic wall and/or hydronephrosis or a nonfunctioning kidney, unless this is known to be due to another cause.
T4Carcinoma has extended beyond the true pelvis and/or has involved (biopsy-proven) the mucosa of the bladder or rectum. Bullous edema alone is not sufficient for T4.

N categories

Clinical

CategoryDefinition
NXRegional lymph nodes cannot be assessed.
N0No regional lymph node metastasis.
N1Regional lymph node metastasis to pelvic lymph nodes only, as detected by imaging and/or pathology (basis for stage IIIC1, with r or p notation).
N2Regional lymph node metastasis to para-aortic lymph nodes, with or without pelvic lymph node involvement (basis for stage IIIC2, with r or p notation).

Pathologic

CategoryDefinition
NXRegional lymph nodes cannot be assessed.
N0No regional lymph node metastasis.
N0(i+)Isolated tumor cells only in regional lymph node(s): 0.2 mm or less, or single cells or clusters of 200 cells or fewer in a single lymph node cross-section. Counted as node-negative and does not upstage.
N1Regional lymph node metastasis to pelvic lymph nodes only.
N1miMicrometastasis to pelvic lymph nodes only: more than 0.2 mm but 2.0 mm or less in greatest dimension.
N1aMacrometastasis to pelvic lymph nodes only: more than 2.0 mm in greatest dimension.
N2Regional lymph node metastasis to para-aortic lymph nodes, with or without positive pelvic lymph nodes.
N2miMicrometastasis to para-aortic lymph nodes: more than 0.2 mm but 2.0 mm or less in greatest dimension, with or without positive pelvic lymph nodes.
N2aMacrometastasis to para-aortic lymph nodes: more than 2.0 mm in greatest dimension, with or without positive pelvic lymph nodes.

M categories

Clinical

CategoryDefinition
M0No distant metastasis.
M1Distant metastasis, including metastasis to inguinal lymph nodes, intraperitoneal disease, lung, liver, or bone. Excludes metastasis to pelvic or para-aortic lymph nodes and to the vagina.

Pathologic

CategoryDefinition
M0No distant metastasis.
M1Microscopically confirmed distant metastasis, including metastasis to inguinal lymph nodes, intraperitoneal disease, lung, liver, or bone; uterine serosa and adnexal involvement also count as M1 for cervical primaries. Excludes metastasis to pelvic or para-aortic lymph nodes and to the vagina.

Notes

Aligned with FIGO 2018: imaging (notation r) and pathology (notation p) may be used to assign stage, especially nodal status (e.g., IIIC1r, IIIC1p). Any pelvic or para-aortic lymph node metastasis makes the case stage IIIC regardless of primary tumor size or extent; isolated tumor cells (N0(i+)) do not upstage. There is no MX category in the 8th edition. Caution: the original AJCC 8th-edition cervix chapter predated final publication of FIGO 2018, so the T1b3 and IIIC1/IIIC2 subdivisions follow FIGO 2018 and were formalized in AJCC version 9. No stage group 0 is assigned (FIGO 2018 has no stage 0) even though Tis exists as a T category.

Reference only — staging must be confirmed against the full AJCC 8th edition chapter and the patient's complete clinical picture.
