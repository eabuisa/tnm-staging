Notes

Clinical and pathologic staging are separate systems in AJCC 8 and give different stage groups, so this site has both stageGroups (clinical) and stageGroupsPathologic. Pathologic N0 requires actual nodal evaluation (e.g., sentinel lymph node biopsy or nodal dissection); nodes that are negative only on clinical exam are cN0 and are staged with the clinical table. There is NO N1c category in AJCC 8 Merkel cell carcinoma (N1c belongs to melanoma of the skin); in-transit disease without nodal metastasis is N2, and in-transit disease with nodal metastasis is N3, in both the clinical and pathologic schemes. Stage 0 is Tis N0 M0 in both tables. Combinations Tis with nodal disease and T0 N0 are not assigned a stage group in AJCC 8 (an in situ tumor cannot metastasize, and T0 N0 is no identifiable cancer) and are excluded from the coverage check. Pathologic Stage IIIA also includes the special case T0 N1b (unknown primary presenting in a clinically apparent node). T0 with occult nodal categories (pN1a(sn)/pN1a) and T0 with in-transit categories (pN2/pN3) are not listed explicitly in published AJCC 8 tables; they are grouped with IIIA and IIIB respectively here so every combination resolves, and this extrapolation is flagged as a simplification.
