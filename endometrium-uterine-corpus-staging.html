Endometrium (Uterine Corpus) — TNM Staging (AJCC 8th)

T, N and M category definitions and stage groups for endometrium (uterine corpus) from the AJCC 8th edition.

T categories

Clinical

CategoryDefinition
TXPrimary tumor cannot be assessed.
T0No evidence of a primary tumor.
T1Tumor is confined to the corpus uteri, including involvement limited to endocervical glands.
T1aTumor is limited to the endometrium or invades less than one half (less than 50%) of the myometrium.
T1bTumor invades one half or more (50% or more) of the myometrium.
T2Tumor invades the stromal connective tissue of the cervix but does not extend beyond the uterus. Involvement of endocervical glands alone does not qualify as T2.
T3Local and/or regional spread beyond the uterus, as specified by T3a and T3b. Pelvic or para-aortic lymph node spread is classified under the N category and results in stage IIIC.
T3aTumor involves the serosa of the corpus uteri and/or the adnexa (fallopian tubes or ovaries), by direct extension or metastasis.
T3bVaginal involvement (by direct extension or metastasis) and/or parametrial involvement.
T4Tumor invades the bladder mucosa and/or the bowel mucosa. Bullous edema alone is not sufficient for T4.

Notes

AJCC 8th corresponds to FIGO 2009. Staging is primarily surgical/pathologic: the clinical lists mirror the same anatomic definitions, but the definitive stage is assigned from operative pathology. Positive peritoneal cytology is reported separately and does not change the stage. Endocervical glandular involvement alone is stage I, not stage II. There is no Tis category and no MX category in AJCC 8th for this site. Note that FIGO published a revised endometrial staging system in 2023 (incorporating histologic type, LVSI, and molecular features); it is not part of AJCC 8th and is not reflected here.

Reference only — staging must be confirmed against the full AJCC 8th edition chapter and the patient's complete clinical picture.
