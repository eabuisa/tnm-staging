Pleural Mesothelioma — TNM Staging (AJCC 8th)

T, N and M category definitions and stage groups for pleural mesothelioma from the AJCC 8th edition.

T categories

Clinical

CategoryDefinition
TXPrimary tumor cannot be assessed.
T0No evidence of a primary tumor.
T1Tumor limited to the pleura on the same side (ipsilateral parietal pleura), with or without involvement of the visceral, mediastinal, or diaphragmatic pleura on that side. In the 8th edition there is a single T1 category — the older T1a/T1b split was dropped.
T2Tumor involving each of the pleural surfaces on the same side (parietal, mediastinal, diaphragmatic, and visceral pleura), plus at least one of: involvement of the diaphragm muscle, or extension from the visceral pleura into the underlying lung tissue (pulmonary parenchyma).
T3Locally advanced but potentially resectable tumor. Tumor involving all of the pleural surfaces on the same side, plus at least one of: involvement of the endothoracic fascia, extension into the mediastinal fat, a solitary, completely resectable focus of tumor extending into the soft tissues of the chest wall, or non-transmural involvement of the pericardium (into, but not through, the pericardium).
T4Locally advanced, technically unresectable tumor. Tumor involving all of the pleural surfaces on the same side, plus at least one of: diffuse extension or multifocal masses in the chest wall (with or without rib destruction), direct extension through the diaphragm to the peritoneum, direct extension to the pleura on the opposite side, direct extension to mediastinal organs, direct extension into the spine or brachial plexus, or tumor extending through to the inner surface of the pericardium (transmural, with or without a pericardial effusion) or involving the myocardium (heart muscle).

N categories

Clinical

CategoryDefinition
NXRegional lymph nodes cannot be assessed.
N0No spread to regional lymph nodes.
N1Spread to lymph nodes on the same side inside the chest: bronchopulmonary, hilar, or mediastinal nodes, including the internal mammary, peridiaphragmatic, pericardial fat pad, and intercostal nodes. In the 8th edition the old N1 and N2 categories were merged into this single N1.
N2Spread to lymph nodes on the opposite side in the chest, in particular contralateral mediastinal nodes, or to supraclavicular (above the collarbone) lymph nodes on either side.

Notes

This chapter applies to diffuse malignant pleural mesothelioma; localized pleural mesothelioma and other primary pleural tumors are not staged with it. In the 8th edition the old T1a/T1b split was dropped (a single T1), and the nodal categories were rebuilt: the old N1 and N2 were merged into a new N1 (same-side intrathoracic nodes), and the old N3 became the new N2 — there is no N3 in the 8th edition. T3 describes locally advanced but potentially resectable tumor and T4 describes technically unresectable tumor. The same TNM descriptors serve clinical and pathologic staging, so the lists are identical.

Reference only — staging must be confirmed against the full AJCC 8th edition chapter and the patient's complete clinical picture.
