Notes

Applies to well-differentiated neuroendocrine tumors (G1, G2, and well-differentiated G3). Poorly differentiated neuroendocrine carcinoma (NEC) is staged using the carcinoma chapter for this organ, not this chapter. Grade (mitotic count and Ki-67 index) is recorded separately and does not change the stage group. Unlike the other GI NET chapters, the stage groups here are subdivided (IIA, IIB, IIIA, IIIB), and T1 (2 cm cutoff) is subdivided into T1a (under 1 cm) and T1b (1–2 cm). For multiple synchronous tumors, use the highest T category and add the (m) suffix (or the tumor count, e.g. T3(2)).

Reference only — staging must be confirmed against the full AJCC 8th edition chapter and the patient's complete clinical picture.
