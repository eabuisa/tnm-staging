T categories

Clinical

CategoryDefinition
TXPrimary tumor cannot be assessed.
T0No evidence of primary tumor.
T1Tumor confined to one vertebral segment or two adjacent vertebral segments.
T2Tumor confined to three adjacent vertebral segments.
T3Tumor confined to four or more adjacent vertebral segments, or to any nonadjacent vertebral segments.
T4Tumor extends into the spinal canal or the great vessels (see T4a and T4b).
T4aTumor extends into the spinal canal.
T4bGross vascular invasion, or tumor thrombus in the great vessels.

N categories

Clinical

CategoryDefinition
NXRegional lymph nodes cannot be assessed. Because nodal spread is rare in bone sarcoma, NX is generally taken to mean N0 unless nodal involvement is clearly evident.
N0No regional lymph node metastasis.

Notes

AJCC 8th edition provides NO prognostic stage groups for spinal bone tumors, so only the T, N, M and grade categories are recorded. A vertebral segment is one of: right vertebral body, left vertebral body, right pedicle, left pedicle, or the posterior element.

Reference only — staging must be confirmed against the full AJCC 8th edition chapter and the patient's complete clinical picture.
