T categories

Clinical

CategoryDefinition
TXPrimary tumor cannot be assessed.
T0No evidence of a primary tumor.
TisCarcinoma in situ - tumor confined to the epithelium, without invasion of deeper layers.
T1Tumor invades into the mucosa beyond the epithelium (lamina propria or submucosa) - see T1a and T1b.
T1aTumor invades the lamina propria.
T1bTumor invades the submucosa.
T2Tumor invades the muscularis propria.
T3Tumor invades through the muscularis propria into the subserosa, or into non-peritonealized perimuscular tissue (mesentery or retroperitoneum) with extension of 2 cm or less.
T4Tumor perforates the visceral peritoneum or directly invades other organs or structures - including other loops of small intestine, mesentery or retroperitoneum more than 2 cm beyond the bowel wall, and the abdominal wall by way of the serosa; for duodenal tumors only, invasion of the pancreas or bile duct is also T4.

Notes

TNM categories are assigned for all small intestine histologies, but these prognostic stage groups apply to adenocarcinoma only. Eighth-edition change: N1 is 1-2 positive nodes and N2 is 3 or more (the 7th edition used 1-3 and 4 or more).
