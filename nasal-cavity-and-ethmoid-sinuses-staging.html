T categories

Clinical

CategoryDefinition
TXPrimary tumor cannot be assessed.
T0No evidence of primary tumor.
TisCarcinoma in situ.
T1Tumor is restricted to any one subsite of the nasal cavity or ethmoid sinuses, with or without bony invasion.
T2Tumor involves two subsites in a single region, or extends to involve an adjacent region within the nasoethmoid complex, with or without bony invasion.
T3Tumor extends to invade the medial wall or floor of the orbit, the maxillary sinus, the palate, or the cribriform plate.
T4aModerately advanced local disease: tumor invades any of the following: the anterior orbital contents, the skin of the nose or cheek, with minimal extension to the anterior cranial fossa, the pterygoid plates, or the sphenoid or frontal sinuses.
T4bVery advanced local disease: tumor invades any of the following: the orbital apex, dura, brain, the middle cranial fossa, cranial nerves other than the maxillary division of the trigeminal nerve (V2), the nasopharynx, or the clivus.

Notes

Applies to carcinomas of the nasal cavity and ethmoid sinuses (the nasoethmoid complex); its T definitions differ from the maxillary sinus chapter even though the N definitions and stage groups are the same. Mucosal melanoma of the sinonasal tract is staged in a different chapter and is not covered here. ENE = extranodal extension. Midline nodes are considered ipsilateral nodes. Clinical and pathologic N use different ENE rules in AJCC 8th edition: clinically, any overt ENE is cN3b; pathologically, ENE in a small single ipsilateral node raises it only to pN2a, while larger or multiple ENE-positive nodes are pN3b. The plain N2 and N3 categories are the parent groupings of their lettered subcategories. One stage table is used for both clinical and pathologic staging at this site. TX and NX cannot be staged, and Tis with nodal spread has no AJCC stage group. T0 with N1 or N2 disease and Tis with nodal spread have no stage group in the AJCC table for this site (T0 N3 disease falls under the 'any T, N3' Stage IVB row).

Reference only — staging must be confirmed against the full AJCC 8th edition chapter and the patient's complete clinical picture.
