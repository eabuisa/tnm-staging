Ovary, Fallopian Tube, and Primary Peritoneum — TNM Staging (AJCC 8th)

T, N and M category definitions and stage groups for ovary, fallopian tube, and primary peritoneum from the AJCC 8th edition.

T categories

Clinical

CategoryDefinition
TXPrimary tumor cannot be assessed.
T0No evidence of a primary tumor.
T1Tumor limited to the ovaries or fallopian tubes (parent category).
T1aTumor limited to one ovary (capsule intact) or one fallopian tube; no tumor on the surface; no malignant cells in ascites or peritoneal washings.
T1bTumor limited to both ovaries (capsules intact) or both fallopian tubes; no tumor on the surface; no malignant cells in ascites or peritoneal washings.
T1cTumor limited to one or both ovaries or fallopian tubes with surgical spill, pre-surgery capsule rupture or surface tumor, or malignant cells in ascites or washings (parent category; see T1c1-T1c3).
T1c1Tumor limited to one or both ovaries or fallopian tubes, with intraoperative surgical spill.
T1c2Tumor limited to one or both ovaries or fallopian tubes, with capsule rupture before surgery or tumor on the ovarian or fallopian tube surface.
T1c3Tumor limited to one or both ovaries or fallopian tubes, with malignant cells in ascites or peritoneal washings.
T2Tumor involves one or both ovaries or fallopian tubes with spread inside the pelvis (below the pelvic brim), or primary peritoneal cancer (parent category).
T2aExtension and/or implants on the uterus and/or fallopian tubes and/or ovaries.
T2bExtension to other tissues inside the pelvis.
T3Tumor involves one or both ovaries or fallopian tubes, or primary peritoneal cancer, with confirmed spread to the peritoneum outside the pelvis (parent category).
T3aMicroscopic peritoneal metastasis outside the pelvis (above the pelvic brim).
T3bMacroscopic peritoneal metastasis outside the pelvis, 2 cm or smaller in greatest dimension.
T3cMacroscopic peritoneal metastasis outside the pelvis, larger than 2 cm in greatest dimension; includes tumor extension to the capsule of the liver or spleen without going into the organ itself.

Clinical

CategoryDefinition

Notes

AJCC 8th edition matches the FIGO 2014 staging for ovary, fallopian tube, and peritoneum, which share one system. Stage IIIA2, IIIB, and IIIC permit NX (nodes not assessed) in the AJCC table. Stage IIIA1 substages are written IIIA1i (N1a) and IIIA1ii (N1b) in FIGO notation.

Reference only — staging must be confirmed against the full AJCC 8th edition chapter and the patient's complete clinical picture.
