T categories

Clinical

CategoryDefinition
TXPrimary tumor cannot be assessed.
T0No evidence of a primary tumor.
TisCarcinoma in situ: intraepithelial carcinoma, or intramucosal carcinoma invading the lamina propria but not through the muscularis mucosae.
T1Tumor invades into the submucosa (through the muscularis mucosae).
T2Tumor invades into the muscularis propria, the thick muscle layer of the bowel wall.
T3Tumor invades through the muscularis propria into the pericolorectal tissues (the fat and connective tissue around the colon or rectum).
T4aTumor penetrates through the surface of the visceral peritoneum (the serosal lining of the bowel).
T4bTumor directly invades, or is adherent to, other organs or structures.

Notes

Colon and rectal cancers share this single AJCC chapter and one set of stage groups. Tumor deposits are discrete cancer nodules in the fat around the bowel without identifiable lymph node tissue; deposits with no positive nodes are N1c. At least 12 regional lymph nodes should be examined for dependable pathologic N staging.

Reference only — staging must be confirmed against the full AJCC 8th edition chapter and the patient's complete clinical picture.
