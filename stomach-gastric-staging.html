Notes

Clinical (cTNM) and pathologic (pTNM) stage groups are different tables in AJCC 8th for stomach: stageGroups below are the clinical groups and stageGroupsPathologic are the pathologic groups. Postneoadjuvant (ypTNM) stage groups also exist in the chapter and are NOT included here. Pathologic staging ideally requires at least 16 regional lymph nodes examined. Note the naming difference: clinically, T4b M0 disease is stage IVA and distant metastasis is stage IVB; pathologically, there is no IVA/IVB split - any M1 disease is stage IV, and T4bN3b M0 is stage IIIC (pathologic).
