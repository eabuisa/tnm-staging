    Anatomic TNM stage groups per the AJCC Cancer Staging Manual, 8th edition. Where a site's stage groups depend on more than T, N, and M (PSA and Grade Group for prostate, age for thyroid, serum markers for testis, mitotic rate for GIST, grade for appendix and the sarcomas), the app asks for those too. A few chapters (soft tissue sarcoma of the head & neck and visceral organs, pelvic and spinal bone tumors, iris and conjunctival melanoma) define T, N, and M categories but no stage groups — for those, the app records the categories and says so. For reference at the point of care — confirm unusual combinations against the manual.
