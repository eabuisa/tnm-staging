CategoryDefinition

Notes

AJCC 8th edition introduced this TNM chapter for thymic epithelial tumors (thymoma and thymic carcinoma); it is based on the IASLC/ITMIG proposal. The T category is set by the deepest structure the tumor directly invades, not by tumor size — there is no size cutoff in the 8th edition. The Masaoka-Koga system is a separate staging system and is still widely used for thymoma; a Masaoka-Koga stage is not interchangeable with the TNM stage group here and should be reported alongside, not instead of, TNM. An M1 tumor should be subcategorized as M1a or M1b before a stage group is assigned. The same T, N, and M descriptors are used for clinical and pathologic staging, so the lists are identical.

Reference only — staging must be confirmed against the full AJCC 8th edition chapter and the patient's complete clinical picture.
