T categories

Clinical

CategoryDefinition
TXPrimary tumor cannot be assessed.
T0No evidence of primary tumor.
T1Tumor 8 cm or smaller in greatest dimension.
T2Tumor larger than 8 cm in greatest dimension.
T3Discontinuous tumors (skip lesions) in the primary bone site.

CategoryDefinition

N categories

Clinical

CategoryDefinition
NXRegional lymph nodes cannot be assessed. Because nodal spread is rare in bone sarcoma, NX is generally taken to mean N0 unless nodal involvement is clearly evident.
N0No regional lymph node metastasis.
N1Regional lymph node metastasis.

Notes

For staging, G1 is low grade and G2/G3 are high grade; GX is grouped with G1. This is the only bone chapter with AJCC prognostic stage groups — there are none for spine or pelvis tumors.

Reference only — staging must be confirmed against the full AJCC 8th edition chapter and the patient's complete clinical picture.
