Notes

Differentiated thyroid carcinoma only (papillary and follicular, as requested); medullary and anaplastic carcinomas use different stage groups and are not included. Stage groups depend on age at diagnosis, with a cutoff of 55 years (raised from 45 in the 7th edition): under 55 there are only stages I (M0) and II (M1); at 55 or older the groups are I, II, III, IVA and IVB. Corrected 55-or-older table, verified against multiple sources: stage I is T1-T2 N0/NX M0 (so T2 N0 is stage I, not stage II), and stage II covers T1-T2 N1 M0 plus T3a/T3b with any N M0 (so T3 with nodal disease is stage II, not stage III); stage III is T4a any N M0 only. In the stage-group matches, parent categories are expanded to their subcategories so an exact selection matches (for example T1 includes T1a and T1b, N0 includes N0a and N0b, and N1 includes N1a and N1b); the parent values T3 and T4 are groupings - T4 itself is not used in a match because T4a and T4b fall in different stages. Only gross extrathyroidal extension affects T category; microscopic/minor extension seen only on histology does not. Tumor size is greatest dimension. Categories may carry (s) solitary or (m) multifocal suffixes in AJCC notation, which are not separate values here. N0a/N0b and N1a/N1b subdivisions do not change the stage group.
