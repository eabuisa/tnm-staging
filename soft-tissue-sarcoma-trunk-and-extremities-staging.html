Notes

Grade is the FNCLCC score: tumor differentiation (1-3) + mitotic count (1-3) + necrosis (0-2); total 2-3 = G1, 4-5 = G2, 6-8 = G3. Unlike the retroperitoneum chapter, regional node metastasis (N1) without distant metastasis is stage IV here. Depth (superficial vs deep) is no longer part of the T category in the 8th edition.

Reference only — staging must be confirmed against the full AJCC 8th edition chapter and the patient's complete clinical picture.
