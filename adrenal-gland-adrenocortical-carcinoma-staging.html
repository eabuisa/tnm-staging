Adrenal Gland (Adrenocortical Carcinoma) — TNM Staging (AJCC 8th)

T, N and M category definitions and stage groups for adrenal gland (adrenocortical carcinoma) from the AJCC 8th edition.

T categories

Clinical

CategoryDefinition
TXPrimary tumor cannot be assessed.
T0No evidence of a primary tumor.
T1Tumor 5 cm or smaller in greatest dimension, with no invasion outside the adrenal gland.
T2Tumor larger than 5 cm in greatest dimension, with no invasion outside the adrenal gland.
T3Tumor of any size with local invasion of surrounding tissue, but not invading neighboring organs.
T4Tumor of any size invading neighboring organs (kidney, diaphragm, pancreas, spleen, or liver) or large blood vessels (renal vein or vena cava), including tumor thrombus in those veins.

Notes

Applies to adrenocortical carcinoma. Note that the ENSAT staging system used in many European guidelines groups some categories differently (for example, ENSAT stage III also includes tumor thrombus or nodal disease with lower T categories); the groups here are the AJCC 8th edition groups.

Reference only — staging must be confirmed against the full AJCC 8th edition chapter and the patient's complete clinical picture.
