T categories

Clinical

CategoryDefinition
TXPrimary tumor cannot be assessed.
T0No evidence of primary tumor.
T1Tumor limited to the iris (see T1a, T1b and T1c).
T1aTumor limited to the iris, no more than 3 clock hours in size.
T1bTumor limited to the iris, more than 3 clock hours in size.
T1cTumor limited to the iris, with secondary glaucoma.
T2Tumor is confluent with, or extends into, the ciliary body, choroid, or both (see T2a, T2b and T2c).
T2aTumor extends into the ciliary body, without secondary glaucoma.
T2bTumor extends into the ciliary body and the choroid, without secondary glaucoma.
T2cTumor extends into the ciliary body, the choroid, or both, with secondary glaucoma.
T3Tumor extends into the ciliary body, choroid, or both, and also into the sclera.
T4Tumor has extrascleral (outside the sclera) extension (see T4a and T4b).
T4aExtrascleral extension of 5 mm or less in largest diameter.
T4bExtrascleral extension of more than 5 mm in largest diameter.

Notes

As in the 7th edition, AJCC 8th edition has NO stage grouping for iris melanoma (per NCI PDQ); T, N and M should still be recorded. If less than half the tumor volume is within the iris, consider that it may have arisen in the ciliary body and classify it as a ciliary body tumor instead.

Reference only — staging must be confirmed against the full AJCC 8th edition chapter and the patient's complete clinical picture.
