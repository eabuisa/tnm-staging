T categories

Clinical

CategoryDefinition
TXPrimary tumor cannot be assessed.
T0No evidence of primary tumor.
T1Tumor confined to one pelvic segment with no extraosseous (soft tissue) extension (see T1a and T1b).
T1aTumor confined to one pelvic segment, no extraosseous extension, 8 cm or smaller in greatest dimension.
T1bTumor confined to one pelvic segment, no extraosseous extension, larger than 8 cm in greatest dimension.
T2Tumor confined to one pelvic segment with extraosseous extension, or to two pelvic segments without extraosseous extension (see T2a and T2b).
T2aTumor in one pelvic segment with extraosseous extension, or two segments without extraosseous extension, 8 cm or smaller in greatest dimension.
T2bTumor in one pelvic segment with extraosseous extension, or two segments without extraosseous extension, larger than 8 cm in greatest dimension.
T3Tumor spanning two pelvic segments with extraosseous extension (see T3a and T3b).
T3aTumor spanning two pelvic segments with extraosseous extension, 8 cm or smaller in greatest dimension.
T3bTumor spanning two pelvic segments with extraosseous extension, larger than 8 cm in greatest dimension.
T4Tumor spanning three pelvic segments or crossing the sacroiliac joint (see T4a and T4b).
T4aTumor involves the sacroiliac joint and extends medial to the sacral neuroforamen.
T4bTumor encases the external iliac vessels or there is gross tumor thrombus in major pelvic vessels.

Notes

AJCC 8th edition provides NO prognostic stage groups for pelvic bone tumors, so only the T, N, M and grade categories are recorded. The four pelvic segments are the sacrum, the iliac wing, the acetabulum/periacetabulum, and the pelvic rami, symphysis and ischium. Note: one staging reference (Pathology Outlines) defines T4a/T4b by the 8 cm size cutoff instead; the definitions used here follow the AJCC 8th/UICC table as reproduced in multiple other sources.

Reference only — staging must be confirmed against the full AJCC 8th edition chapter and the patient's complete clinical picture.
