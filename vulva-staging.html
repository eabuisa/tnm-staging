Vulva — TNM Staging (AJCC 8th)

T, N and M category definitions and stage groups for vulva from the AJCC 8th edition.

T categories

Clinical

CategoryDefinition
TXPrimary tumor cannot be assessed.
T0No evidence of a primary tumor.
TisCarcinoma in situ (preinvasive carcinoma). AJCC includes a stage 0 for this; FIGO no longer does.
T1Tumor confined to the vulva and/or perineum (parent category; use T1a or T1b when size and invasion depth are known).
T1aLesion 2 cm or smaller, confined to the vulva and/or perineum, with stromal invasion of 1.0 mm or less.
T1bLesion larger than 2 cm, or a lesion of any size with stromal invasion of more than 1.0 mm, confined to the vulva and/or perineum.
T2Tumor of any size extending to nearby perineal structures: the lower (distal) third of the urethra, the lower (distal) third of the vagina, or the anus.
T3Tumor of any size extending to the upper (proximal) two thirds of the urethra, the upper (proximal) two thirds of the vagina, the bladder lining, or the rectal lining, or tumor fixed to the pelvic bone.

Notes

AJCC 8th edition vulvar staging matches the FIGO 2009 system (including the 5 mm nodal size cutoffs). FIGO revised vulvar staging again in 2021 and AJCC has since moved to a version 9 protocol with different T and N definitions; this entry is the 8th edition. Depth of invasion is measured from the epithelial-stromal junction of the nearest superficial dermal papilla to the deepest point of invasion. The parent N2 category is not assigned to a single stage group because N2a/N2b are stage IIIB while N2c is stage IIIC; select the subcategory.

Reference only — staging must be confirmed against the full AJCC 8th edition chapter and the patient's complete clinical picture.
