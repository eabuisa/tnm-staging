Oral Cavity — TNM Staging (AJCC 8th)

T, N and M category definitions and stage groups for oral cavity from the AJCC 8th edition.

T categories

Clinical

CategoryDefinition
TXPrimary tumor cannot be assessed.
TisCarcinoma in situ.
T1Tumor 2 cm or smaller in greatest dimension, with depth of invasion (DOI) 5 mm or less. DOI is depth of invasion, not tumor thickness.
T2Tumor 2 cm or smaller with DOI more than 5 mm but no more than 10 mm, or tumor more than 2 cm but no more than 4 cm with DOI 10 mm or less.
T3Tumor more than 2 cm but no more than 4 cm with DOI more than 10 mm, or tumor more than 4 cm with DOI 10 mm or less. Often summarized as a tumor more than 4 cm or any tumor with DOI more than 10 mm; a tumor more than 4 cm with DOI more than 10 mm is T4a.
T4aModerately advanced local disease: tumor more than 4 cm with DOI more than 10 mm, or tumor invades adjacent structures only (for example, through the cortical bone of the mandible or maxilla, or involves the maxillary sinus or the skin of the face).
T4bVery advanced local disease: tumor invades the masticator space, pterygoid plates, or skull base, and/or encases the internal carotid artery.

N categories

Clinical

CategoryDefinition
NXRegional lymph nodes cannot be assessed.
N0No regional lymph node metastasis.
N1Metastasis in a single ipsilateral lymph node, 3 cm or smaller in greatest dimension, with no extranodal extension (ENE-negative).
N2aMetastasis in a single ipsilateral lymph node, more than 3 cm but no more than 6 cm in greatest dimension, with no extranodal extension (ENE-negative).
N2bMetastases in multiple ipsilateral lymph nodes, none more than 6 cm in greatest dimension, with no extranodal extension (ENE-negative).
N2cMetastases in bilateral or contralateral lymph nodes, none more than 6 cm in greatest dimension, with no extranodal extension (ENE-negative).
N3aMetastasis in a lymph node more than 6 cm in greatest dimension, with no extranodal extension (ENE-negative).
N3bMetastasis in any lymph node(s) with clinically overt extranodal extension (ENE-positive).

Pathologic

CategoryDefinition
NXRegional lymph nodes cannot be assessed.
N0No regional lymph node metastasis.
N1Metastasis in a single ipsilateral lymph node, 3 cm or smaller in greatest dimension, with no extranodal extension (ENE-negative).
N2aMetastasis in a single ipsilateral lymph node 3 cm or smaller in greatest dimension with extranodal extension (ENE-positive), or a single ipsilateral lymph node more than 3 cm but no more than 6 cm in greatest dimension with no extranodal extension (ENE-negative).
N2bMetastases in multiple ipsilateral lymph nodes, none more than 6 cm in greatest dimension, with no extranodal extension (ENE-negative).
N2cMetastases in bilateral or contralateral lymph nodes, none more than 6 cm in greatest dimension, with no extranodal extension (ENE-negative).
N3aMetastasis in a lymph node more than 6 cm in greatest dimension, with no extranodal extension (ENE-negative).
N3bMetastasis in a single ipsilateral lymph node more than 3 cm in greatest dimension with extranodal extension (ENE-positive), or multiple ipsilateral, contralateral, or bilateral lymph nodes, any with ENE-positive, or a single contralateral lymph node of any size with ENE-positive.

Notes

Depth of invasion (DOI) is measured from the basement membrane of the nearest normal mucosa to the deepest point of tumor invasion and is not the same as tumor thickness. Superficial erosion of bone or a tooth socket (alone) by a gingival primary tumor is not sufficient to classify the tumor as T4. ENE = extranodal extension; clinically overt ENE is classified cN3b.
