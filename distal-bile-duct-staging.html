T categories

Clinical

CategoryDefinition
TXPrimary tumor cannot be assessed.
TisCarcinoma in situ / high-grade dysplasia.
T1Tumor invades the bile duct wall with a depth of invasion of less than 5 mm.
T2Tumor invades the bile duct wall with a depth of invasion of 5-12 mm.
T3Tumor invades the bile duct wall with a depth of invasion of more than 12 mm.
T4Tumor involves the celiac axis, superior mesenteric artery, and/or common hepatic artery.

Notes

New in the 8th edition: the T category is based on the measured depth of invasion (less than 5 mm, 5-12 mm, more than 12 mm) instead of the layer-based 7th edition definitions, and invasion of adjacent organs such as the pancreas, duodenum, or gallbladder no longer determines the T category.

Reference only — staging must be confirmed against the full AJCC 8th edition chapter and the patient's complete clinical picture.
