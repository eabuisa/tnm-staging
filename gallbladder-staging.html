Notes

New in the 8th edition: T2 is split by tumor side - T2a on the peritoneal (free serosal) side is stage IIA, while T2b on the hepatic side is stage IIB. The N category is now based on the number of positive nodes (N1 = 1-3, N2 = 4 or more), replacing the location-based N categories of the 7th edition.
